# Photos

Drop your site photos in this folder.

## Naming suggestions

- `gallery-1.jpg`, `gallery-2.jpg` ... — gallery section images
- `hero-bg.jpg` — hero background photo (if replacing the brick pattern)
- `joe.jpg` — photo of Joe for the About section

## Recommended sizes

- Gallery photos: at least 800px wide, landscape or portrait both work
- Keep file sizes under 500KB where possible (use https://squoosh.app to compress)

Once photos are in this folder, ask Claude Code to wire them into the gallery section.
